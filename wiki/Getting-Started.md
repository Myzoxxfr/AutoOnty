# Getting Started with AutoOnty

Here's how to quickly get up and running with AutoOnty!

---

## 1. How It Works

AutoOnty is completely portable:

- **No Installation:** Download and run it from any folder. No setup wizards or administrator rights needed.
- **Single Window:** If AutoOnty is already open and you launch it again, it brings your existing window to the front.
- **Saves Your Settings:** Hotkeys, speed, loops, and theme are saved automatically to `AutoOnty.ini` right next to the executable.
- **Safe Window Position:** If you disconnect a secondary monitor, the window automatically recenters onto your active display.

---

## 2. Interface and Buttons

The AutoOnty toolbar provides quick access to all essential actions:

- **Macros:** Open your macro library (stored in `Documents\AutoOnty`) to load, edit, rename or delete saved macros, give them a load hotkey, or set their own loops / speed.
- **Save:** Recordings are not saved automatically. Click the save icon at the bottom left of the window (or press <kbd>Ctrl+S</kbd>), name your macro, and it is saved as a `.onty` file.
- **Record:** Click to start recording (turns into a white square while recording; click again to stop).
- **Play:** Click to play your macro (turns into a white square while playing; click again to cancel).
- **Autoclick:** Start or stop the built-in autoclicker (see [Autoclick](Autoclick.md)).
- **Settings:** Open settings for speed multipliers, playback loops, autoclick, themes, custom hotkeys, and language.

---

## 3. How to Record & Play

### Recording a Macro

1. Press the Record hotkey (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Alt+R</kbd> by default) or click **Record**.
2. AutoOnty starts capturing your keyboard and mouse actions.
3. Perform your workflow or game actions.
4. Press the Record hotkey again (or click Stop). The final stop shortcut is automatically omitted from the macro.

### Playing It Back

1. Press the Play hotkey (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+Alt+P</kbd> by default) or click **Play**.
2. The cursor moves to your starting position, and actions replay smoothly.
3. AutoOnty repeats the macro for your chosen number of loops.
4. **Need to stop?** Press your panic stop key (default <kbd>Pause</kbd>, rebindable) or move your physical mouse!
